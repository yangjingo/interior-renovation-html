# Anonymized design revision example

This note records the design decisions and review workflow without reproducing the client's drawings, dimensions, title blocks, construction details, or renders. The changes below are **concept proposals**, not approved construction revisions.

| Area | Earlier concept | Revised concept | Drawings still needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Second-floor northwest room and adjoining balcony | Bedroom beside a separate laundry balcony | Open electric light-preparation kitchen; a bar continues into the inner balcony bay | Architect: room use, openings and enclosure. Structural designer: wall, beam and balcony edge. Water/drainage designer: supply, waste, waterproofing and exhaust coordination. Electrical designer: appliance load and dedicated circuits. |
| Third-floor wet-area group | Two separate bathrooms; a later, now superseded concept merged them | Retain one shared bathroom. Convert the former bathroom adjoining the primary bedroom into a dry walk-in closet with bedroom access. | Architect: room use, doors and ventilation. Structural designer: proposed bedroom opening and adjacent wall/slab. Water/drainage designer: retain the shared bathroom and detail safe closure and access for former fixtures and pipes. Electrical designer: separate bathroom and closet lighting, sockets and ventilation. |
| Upper-floor exterior terrace | Exterior terrace | Keep it roofless as a small garden with movable lightweight planting and clear access to drains and guards. | Structural designer: planting and wet-soil load. Architect and water/drainage designer: thresholds, guard, waterproofing, falls, drains and overflow. Electrical designer: outdoor-rated lighting and outlets. |

The comparison UI shows each affected floor's whole-floor **earlier/current** views first, followed by room details and construction-drawing review. The merged-bathroom render is now historical, while the bathroom/closet and open-garden previews communicate the current concept. Generated images cannot establish a pipe route, circuit capacity, drain level, planting load or safe demolition boundary. Keep a proposed change pending until the relevant professional issues a coordinated revision and the site conditions are checked.

The private source PDFs, drawing crops, detailed HTML review and render workspace are intentionally outside this public note. Apply the reusable [drawing change review](../references/drawing-change-review.md) to the actual, access-controlled project files.
