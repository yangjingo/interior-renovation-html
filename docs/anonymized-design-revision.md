# Anonymized design revision example

This note records the design decisions and review workflow without reproducing the client's drawings, dimensions, title blocks, construction details, or renders. The changes below are **concept proposals**, not approved construction revisions.

| Area | Earlier concept | Revised concept | Drawings still needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Second-floor northwest room and adjoining balcony | Bedroom beside a separate laundry balcony | Open electric light-preparation kitchen; a bar continues into the inner balcony bay | Architect: room use, openings and enclosure. Structural designer: wall, beam and balcony edge. Water/drainage designer: supply, waste, waterproofing and exhaust coordination. Electrical designer: appliance load and dedicated circuits. |
| Third-floor wet-area group | Two separate bathrooms | One larger bathroom with one entrance, dry vanity/toilet zone and a glass-separated shower zone | Architect: coordinated room and door layout. Structural designer: intermediate wall, beam and slab. Water/drainage designer: stacks, drain levels and waterproofing. Electrical designer: wet-area lighting, sockets and ventilation points. |

The comparison UI shows each affected floor's whole-floor **earlier/current** views first, followed by room details and construction-drawing review. Historical renders remain labelled as historical concepts; generated images cannot establish a pipe route, circuit capacity, drain level or safe demolition boundary. Keep a proposed change pending until the relevant professional issues a coordinated revision and the site conditions are checked.

The private source PDFs, drawing crops, detailed HTML review and render workspace are intentionally outside this public note. Apply the reusable [drawing change review](../references/drawing-change-review.md) to the actual, access-controlled project files.
