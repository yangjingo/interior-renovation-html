# Anonymized design revision example

This note records the design decisions and review workflow without reproducing the client's drawings, dimensions, title blocks, construction details, or renders. The changes below are **concept proposals**, not approved construction revisions.

## Circulation review

The latest private visual review compares the earlier and revised room previews in five pairs: the second-floor overall view and kitchen/bar detail, then the third-floor overall view, primary-bedroom closet detail and open-garden detail. The comparison is a design communication aid, not a dimensioned circulation or construction plan.

- Second floor: reduce the island's visual footprint and tuck stools under the window bar so movement from the stair landing through the kitchen remains legible. Keep the family lounge route to its balcony separate from the bar seating zone.
- Third floor: keep the shared bathroom accessible from the common area. The proposed dry closet serves only the exterior-window primary bedroom; bed, wardrobe and closet access must be checked together.
- Exterior garden: keep movable seating and planting away from the central activity and drain-maintenance route. Its actual entrance and any alteration to the exterior boundary remain unresolved until the architect and relevant specialists confirm them.

The private HTML files include floor-specific renders and drawing crops. They are not reproduced in this public repository; this note records only the anonymized decisions and unresolved checks.
